1/5 as a Percent and Decimal

Fractions, decimals, and percentages are different ways to represent the same value. Learning how to convert fractions into decimals and percentages helps in solving mathematical problems, performing financial calculations, and understanding measurements. In this article, we will learn how to convert 1/5 into a decimal and percent, along with step-by-step methods and examples.

Understanding the Fraction 1/5

The fraction 1/5 represents one part out of five equal parts of a whole.

Breaking Down 1/5

  • 1 (Numerator): Represents the selected or used part.
  • 5 (Denominator): Represents the total number of equal parts.

For example, if a pizza is divided into 5 equal slices and you eat 1 slice, you have eaten 1/5 of the pizza.

How to Convert 1/5 into a Decimal

To convert a fraction into a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator.

Step-by-Step Method

Step 1: Write the Fraction as Division

1 ÷ 5

Step 2: Perform the Division

1 ÷ 5 = 0.2

So, 1/5 as a decimal is 0.2.

How to Convert 1/5 into a Percent

To convert a decimal into a percent, multiply the decimal by 100.

Step-by-Step Method

Step 1: Use the Decimal Value

Decimal form of 1/5 = 0.2

Step 2: Multiply by 100

0.2 × 100 = 20%

So, 1/5 as a percent is 20%.

Visual Representation of 1/5

Understanding fractions visually makes learning easier.

Imagine a chocolate bar divided into 5 equal pieces. If you eat 1 piece, you have consumed:

  • Fraction: 1/5
  • Decimal: 0.2
  • Percent: 20%

This means you consumed 20% of the chocolate bar.

Why Converting 1/5 Is Important

Fraction conversions are used in many real-life situations.

Cooking and Baking

If a recipe requires 1/5 cup of sugar, it equals 0.2 cup or 20% of one cup.

Time Calculations

1/5 of one hour equals:

60 × 1/5 = 12 minutes

Financial Calculations

If you spend 1/5 of your budget, it means you spent 20% of your total money.

Alternative Method to Convert 1/5 into Percent

Another way to convert fractions into percentages is by multiplying the fraction directly by 100.

Step 1: Multiply Fraction by 100

(1/5) × 100 = 100/5

Step 2: Solve the Division

100 ÷ 5 = 20%

Common Mistakes While Converting Fractions

Dividing Denominator by Numerator

Always divide numerator by denominator when converting fractions into decimals.

Forgetting to Multiply by 100

After converting to decimal, multiply by 100 to get percentage.

Real-Life Examples of 1/5 Conversion

Food Portions

Eating 1 slice from 5 slices of pizza means you ate 20% of the pizza.

Academic Scores

If a student completes 1 out of 5 questions, they completed 20% of the test.

Shopping and Discounts

If you receive a 1/5 discount, it means you save 20% on the purchase.
